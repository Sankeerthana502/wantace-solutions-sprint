## 1. 5-Minute Evaluation Video Script & Cue Card
* **Recording Guidelines:** Record on Loom, Zoom, or smartphone camera. Keep camera enabled throughout. Speak naturally in English or conversational Hinglish. Do not read verbatim—use these bulleted timestamps as natural mental milestones.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| VIDEO STRUCTURE (5 MINUTES TOTAL)                                             |
| 0:00 - 1:30 | Section 1: Surprises from the Real-Business Audit               |
| 1:30 - 3:15 | Section 2: What the Client Didn't Say Directly in the Data Pack |
| 3:15 - 5:00 | Section 3: The One Decision I Would Change with Another 24 Hours|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 0:00 – 1:30 | What Surprised Me During the Real-Business Audit
* **Hook & Intro:** *"Hello, I am [Your Name], candidate for the Solutions Engineer Intern role at Wantace."*
* **The Core Surprise:** *"When auditing a sheet-metal fabrication workshop for Part A, my biggest shock wasn’t that they lacked modern software—it was how remarkably sophisticated their informal, manual coping systems actually were. The owner maintained an intricate colour-coded chalk numbering system on bent sheet panels."*
* **The Insight:** *"This revealed a fundamental Solutions Engineering reality: Small manufacturers don’t resist digital tools because they fear technology; they resist because enterprise software often breaks fast, intuitive physical coping mechanisms in exchange for slow, rigid desktop forms. Factory bottlenecks are information-routing problems, not machine capacity problems."*

### 1:30 – 3:15 | The Most Important Things the Client Didn't Say Directly
* **The Surface vs Ground Reality:** *"Analyzing Vardhan Steelware’s client pack revealed several critical truths that neither Suresh nor Kunal stated directly on the call."*
* **Denial on Payments:** *"Suresh explicitly said 'Payment koi problem nahi. Purani parties hain.' But the order sheet and WhatsApp logs proved that Sharma Bartan Bhandar owes ₹1.8 Lakh in old dues, had two orders held in just one week, and was aggressively chasing stock for Diwali. Suresh is in denial about credit leakage because they are legacy relationships."*
* **The Myth of Ramesh's All-Knowing Diary:** *"Suresh claimed 'Ramesh ki diary mein sab hai.' But the WhatsApp export showed Ramesh himself asking Kunal: 'Bhagona sheet mein do jagah likha hai? Gupta ka 40 aur New Delhi ka 60, dono polish mein hain na?' Even Ramesh is operating in the dark."*
* **Contractor Shrinkage Masked as 'Wastage':** *"Suresh brushed off a 3–4% steel difference as normal wastage. But WhatsApp showed Karigar Pappu took 420 kg, returned 1,180 pieces, with a 6 kg shortage. That’s hundreds of kilograms of high-grade steel bleeding into third-party workshops every month."*
* **Misplaced Priorities:** *"Kunal wanted AI forecasting and Amazon/Flipkart integration for a channel that represents only 12% of orders, completely ignoring that 88% of their business comes from wholesale distributors in Ambala, Karnal, and Panipat."*

### 3:15 – 5:00 | The One Decision I Would Change with Another 24 Hours
* **The Self-Reflection:** *"If I had an extra 24 hours to refine my requirement document, the primary decision I would revisit is our external polishing contractor interface."*
* **The Trade-Off Made:** *"In my Phase 1 scope, I excluded contractors from the software entirely, having Ramesh log gate returns manually on a tablet to ensure adoption. While safe, this still leaves an information blindspot while goods are sitting on the contractor's floor."*
* **The Advanced Solution:** *"With one more day, I would architect a headless WhatsApp conversational webhook for the contractor. The vendor wouldn’t log into any app; they would simply text a photo of their weighment slip or type two numbers into WhatsApp. An automated OCR parse would pre-populate Ramesh’s gate screen, cutting check-in time to zero without introducing adoption resistance."*
* **Closing:** *"Thank you for your time, and I look forward to discussing this in detail."*
